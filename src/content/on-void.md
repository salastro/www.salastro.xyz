 I do not know why I felt the need to write this post. I have been using Void
 for 5-6 years. For the most part, I have enjoyed this journey with its ups and
 downs. It was sometimes challenging to work around runit as everything is
 built around systemd and even more was musl as I when I started it was my main
 base rather than the glibc version. Admittedly, this helped me improve my
 technical skills and deep understanding way more than otherwise. That said,
 Void has been recently insufferable. Contributing to the packages has been
 more annoying than ever as my PRs get closed too quickly without properly
 addressing the content; frequent failures in the mirror repos (I am unsure
 whether this has been a widespread issue to anyone other than me); and
 unfixable bad behaviour and some bugs here and there. The recent issue in
 noto-fonts made me open the subreddit looking for answers then I saw a
 submission mentioning the recent maintainer's orphanage of 100+ packages over
 an AI dispute. I believed it was some kind of AI code slop nonsense that does
 not deserve to be looked at (as I am very anti-LLM generated code in that
 sense), yet to my surprise, it was just a comment. The comment was simply
 noting breaks introduced by a version bump. I believe this is wholeheartedly
 dumb and overly pedantic. Sadly, this place is no longer meant for me as this
 distro is hardly functional anymore. I know no one will care and no one is
 supposed to; this is more like a personal sacrifice as I reminiscent on the
 days I used to make videos talking all about Void.
